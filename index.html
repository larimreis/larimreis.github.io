🔁 Why Seeds Matter

A seed is simply a starting number. In Skyline Series, that number drives every decision behind a composition: how many buildings appear, how tall they grow, where the window grid falls, how spires reach upward, and how stars spread across the sky.

Because the system is deterministic, the same seed always produces the same artwork. You can share a seed with someone else and they will see exactly what you see. That is what makes the series a true reproducible catalogue rather than a collection of one-off images.

🧵 Textile and Pattern Use

The gridded facades and repeated window shapes in Skyline Series translate naturally into woven and printed pattern work. Designers use the compositions as starting points for fabric layouts, surface repeats, and colour studies.

Each theme offers a different density of detail. Some lean toward bold, blocky silhouettes. Others favour fine vertical rhythm and delicate star scatter. Exploring several seeds within one theme is a quick way to build a small library of pattern references.
